# TrustReady — Constraints

## Evidence integrity
- every verified control must link to accepted evidence or explicit authorised attestation;
- evidence freshness/version must remain visible;
- unknown evidence remains unknown;
- reproducibility is preferred over persuasive prose.

## Claim boundary
A 100/100 score means all controls in the selected readiness profile have accepted evidence/attestation. It does **not** mean universal legal compliance, certification, zero risk or government approval.

## Security / commercial
- paid capabilities require valid entitlements;
- API/MCP boundaries must not let model-generated intent bypass authorization;
- sensitive evidence must not leak into public trust-centre outputs.

## Frameworks
Mappings may reference EU AI Act, GDPR evidence, NIST AI RMF/GenAI Profile, ISO/IEC 42001, BSI-oriented controls and buyer questionnaires without claiming to replace formal assessment.
