# Intent

## One sentence

Help a developer, company or public-sector buyer decide whether an AI system is ready to buy/deploy/approve by making every readiness claim inspectable, reproducible and paired with the exact action that closes the gap.

## Primary users

- developers preparing an AI product for serious buyers;
- security/privacy/governance teams evaluating evidence;
- procurement/public-sector buyers who need decision-grade assurance rather than a marketing score.

## Problem

AI readiness is spread across repositories, policies, tests, security controls, privacy evidence, operations and questionnaires. Existing trust/compliance answers often collapse this into a badge or opaque score that the buyer must simply trust.

## Desired outcome

A buyer can inspect what was required, what evidence was checked, what was observed, what remains unknown, how fresh it is, how to reproduce the result and exactly how to close every gap.

## Non-goals

- universal legal compliance certification;
- claiming government approval;
- a magic AI trust score detached from evidence;
- allowing AI-assisted evidence discovery to become final control authority;
- treating missing evidence as evidence of absence or presence.

## North star

**TrustReady should be useful even if the buyer does not trust TrustReady.**

## Success looks like

Two independent reviewers using the same evidence snapshot + rule/profile version can reproduce the material readiness result, inspect disagreements and see a concrete path from current gaps to the selected profile's accepted evidence state.
