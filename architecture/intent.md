# TrustReady — Intent

## Problem
Teams and buyers struggle to answer whether an AI system is ready to buy, deploy or approve. Checklists and opaque scores often hide which evidence was actually inspected, what remains unknown and how to reproduce the result.

## Outcome
Produce an evidence-backed readiness view where every control can show requirement, rationale, inspected evidence, observed state, unknowns, freshness, reproducibility and the exact remediation step.

## Users
- AI developers preparing for procurement;
- companies reviewing vendors/internal systems;
- public-sector procurement, security, privacy and AI-governance reviewers.

## North star
**Useful even when the buyer does not trust TrustReady.** Results should be independently inspectable and reproducible.

## Non-goals
- magic universal compliance score;
- replacing certification, auditors or legal advice;
- equating 100/100 with zero risk or government approval.
