# TrustReady GTM Validation Loop

Definition of done for this phase:

- 30-50 candidate-rich public repositories scanned.
- Top candidates ranked by security confidence and commercial fit.
- Top 10 human-reviewed before any contact.
- Only 3-5 candidates with defensible source evidence and plausible buyer value receive outreach drafts.
- No automated sending.
- Success is measured by replies, calls, paid validation interest and ultimately a paying customer — not by scanner finding count.
