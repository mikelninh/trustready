# Prospect Security Signal — first batch operating note

This sprint does not claim product-market fit from source scans alone. The source scan is only a way to produce higher-quality outreach.

## Human review checklist before contacting a maintainer

1. Confirm the repository is the maintainer/team you intend to contact.
2. Re-open the cited files at the exact revision and confirm the effect signal is not only a test/example/docs path.
3. Confirm wording says **source signal / proof gap**, not vulnerability, unless authorized runtime evidence exists.
4. If the issue looks severe and credibly exploitable, stop the sales motion and use responsible disclosure.
5. Do not mention or imply negative consequences for refusing to buy. Describe only conditional technical/business impact if the control is actually insufficient.
6. Keep the free signal useful even if they never pay.

## Metrics to record

For each lead: signal sent, reply, technical call, owned-CI permission, finding confirmed/rejected, remediation accepted, CI retained, amount paid, reason lost.

Target proof: 5 specific signals sent → 3 technical conversations → 2 validations/CI retention → 1 paid customer.
