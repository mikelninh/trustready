# TrustReady

**Evidence-backed AI procurement readiness — every claim inspectable, every gap actionable.**

TrustReady helps developers, companies and public-sector buyers answer a hard question:

> Can we trust this AI system enough to buy, deploy or approve it — and what evidence supports that answer?

TrustReady does **not** issue a magic compliance score. It builds a reproducible evidence graph across governance, security, privacy, AI risk, transparency, human oversight, testing, operations and procurement controls.

## Core promise

For every control, TrustReady must show:

1. **What is required**
2. **Why it matters**
3. **What evidence was inspected**
4. **What was observed**
5. **What remains unknown**
6. **How fresh the evidence is**
7. **How the result can be reproduced**
8. **What exact action closes the gap**

A `100/100` readiness score means **all controls in the selected readiness profile are backed by accepted evidence or explicit authorised attestation**. It does **not** mean universal legal compliance, certification, zero risk, or government approval.

## End-state product

```text
connect sources
  ↓
collect immutable evidence
  ↓
normalise + hash + timestamp
  ↓
deterministic control checks
  ↓
optional AI-assisted evidence discovery (never final authority)
  ↓
readiness graph + gaps
  ↓
remediation engine
  ↓
re-scan
  ↓
verifiable buyer pack / trust centre / API
  ↓
continuous monitoring + expiry
```

## Trust principle

**TrustReady should be useful even if the buyer does not trust TrustReady.**

Every verified result must be independently inspectable and reproducible from its evidence and rule version.

## Reference frameworks

TrustReady is designed to map controls to multiple frameworks without pretending to replace formal certification or legal advice, including:

- EU AI Act
- GDPR / data-protection evidence
- NIST AI RMF + GenAI Profile
- ISO/IEC 42001 mappings
- BSI-oriented AI/cloud assurance mappings where applicable
- customer-specific procurement questionnaires
