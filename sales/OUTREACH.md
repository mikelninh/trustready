# TrustReady Founding Pilot — Outreach Pack

## Positioning rule

Do **not** lead with "AI security scanner", compliance, or fear.

Lead with one inspectable engineering question:

> **Can your agent reach a real effect it should not be able to reach — and can you prove the guard stops it without breaking legitimate work?**

The offer is a bounded 7-day engineering proof, not a certification pitch.

## Core proof

Use this exact claim:

> Across four real repositories, our controlled Security Delta benchmark measured 14/14 named attack inputs reaching impact when the relevant boundary was weakened, and 0/14 after the real boundary was restored. Six frozen holdouts were contained and 7/7 benign controls still worked.

Always follow with the boundary:

> Those are named controlled scenarios, not a penetration test or claim that unknown vulnerabilities do not exist.

## Outreach operating rule

First wave: **8 highly personalized messages**, not 30 generic blasts.

After 8 sends:

- if replies >= 2: continue the same message family;
- if replies = 0–1: rewrite the first paragraph and offer before increasing volume;
- if discovery calls happen but no pilot closes: inspect scope/pricing friction rather than sending more cold email;
- never claim certification, universal security, or production penetration-test equivalence.
